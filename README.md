# Sidedrop

Send files straight from one device to another, right in the browser — no app, no cloud, no
account.

Sidedrop moves files between two devices in real time. Open it in the browser on both — say, your
phone and your laptop — pair them with a link, a short code or a QR code, and drop in files, whole
folders or a piece of text. They arrive on the other device as you watch.

Your files travel directly from one device to the other, encrypted along the way. Nothing is
uploaded to the cloud and nothing is kept anywhere. It works even when the two devices are on
different networks, such as a phone on mobile data and a laptop on Wi-Fi, and there is no
artificial limit on file size.

Sidedrop is free and open source, with no ads and no sign-up. This repository holds the Sidedrop
app that runs in your browser.
